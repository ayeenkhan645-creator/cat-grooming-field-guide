Evidence and further reading

Sources for gentle coat care.

Our guides are practical summaries, not clinical instructions. The following veterinary and welfare references provide important context and safety boundaries.

Veterinary references

VCA Animal Hospitals — Grooming and Coat Care for Your Cat explains brush selection, coat maintenance, matting hazards and why most healthy cats do not need frequent bathing.

VCA Animal Hospitals — How to Trim a Cat’s Nails describes gradual paw-handling training and the importance of avoiding the quick.

2022 AAFP/ISFM Cat Friendly Veterinary Interaction Guidelines supports choice, recognizing feline emotional state and minimizing distress during handling.

Welfare and science

Cats Protection — Cat Body Language provides a broader framework for reading ears, body posture and tail movements instead of relying on one signal.

Cats Protection — Grooming is a practical welfare resource on coat care and brushing.

Editorial method

We organize the guides around individual observations and everyday welfare questions. Our pages explain when owners should stop, seek a veterinarian or request help from a trained cat groomer. Sources can change, and any treatment decision should follow advice from a qualified veterinary professional familiar with the cat.

Our illustrations are original, simplified artwork and are not anatomical diagrams. This guide is independent and does not claim affiliation with any veterinary organization listed above.

Cat Grooming Field Guide

Kind, practical coat-care education for people who share a home with a cat. Every cat is an individual; grooming is never a reason to override a clear refusal.
