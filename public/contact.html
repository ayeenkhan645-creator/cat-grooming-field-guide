Need advice for an individual cat?

Ask your veterinarian if your cat has painful tangles, sudden coat deterioration, broken skin, unusual hair loss or difficulty moving. For severe distress during handling, a veterinarian or qualified cat-behavior professional can help you develop an appropriate plan. Please do not send personal medical histories through unverified contact methods.

Cat Grooming Field Guide

Kind, practical coat-care education for people who share a home with a cat. Every cat is an individual; grooming is never a reason to override a clear refusal.
