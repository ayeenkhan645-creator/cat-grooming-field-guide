Why this site exists

Practical grooming, without pressure.

A small independent educational project designed around the cat's comfort and the owner's confidence.

What we believe

Coat care should be calm, realistic and individualized. There is no universal grooming schedule that fits every cat. Coat texture, movement, health, home environment and learned handling preferences all affect what works. Our guides encourage voluntary participation and careful observation instead of restraining a frightened cat to complete a checklist.

What this site offers

The library contains thirteen practical guides with everyday examples, important safety boundaries and small activities you can try without forcing your cat. An optional browser-based planner can help you notice patterns over time. It does not send entries to a database and is intended for personal observations rather than medical records.

Editorial boundaries

We do not diagnose or treat illness, recommend a grooming product as a substitute for veterinary advice, or encourage unsafe home mat removal. For new pain, spreading skin disease, severe tangles or potential exposure to toxic substances, ask a veterinary professional. The site references independent sources for context, with a dedicated page listing them.

Updates and corrections

We aim to keep guides accurate and easy to understand. If you find a broken source, misleading wording or an accessibility issue, use our corrections page. We welcome suggestions for new subjects based on genuine needs of cat owners, not link placement requests.

Cat Grooming Field Guide

Kind, practical coat-care education for people who share a home with a cat. Every cat is an individual; grooming is never a reason to override a clear refusal.
