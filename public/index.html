Our approach

Care is not a contest.

Brushing can support coat comfort and help owners notice changes, but it should never require intimidating restraint. Our guides distinguish ordinary maintenance from situations best handled by a veterinarian or qualified groomer. The descriptions are educational rather than diagnoses, and breed-like appearance is not a substitute for examining the individual animal.

For related learning, visit the independent Indoor Cat Enrichment Lab for environment and activity ideas, or the Cat Sleep Observatory for gentle sleep-observation notes.
